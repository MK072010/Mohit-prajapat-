How much does a website cost?

It depends on pages and features. Send me your idea on WhatsApp and I will share a clear quote before any work starts.

A simple business site is usually quicker than people expect. I will give you a timeline when we discuss your requirements.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site includes SEO basics: page titles, descriptions, sitemap and fast loading. Local SEO and Google Business Profile are available as add-ons.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. I design phone-first and test on desktop after.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It is hosted on Vercel and can be connected to your own domain.
